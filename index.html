04 — Selected Works

Ideas turned into reality.

PlastiTrack

View on GitHub

PlastiTrack is a personal project I created after seeing how plastic waste is often poorly managed in my community. Although many people are willing to recycle, there is no simple, organized way to connect them with waste collectors, resulting in recyclable plastics ending up in dumpsites or the environment. To address this, I built PlastiTrack—a web-based plastic waste logistics platform that makes recycling more accessible, organized, and efficient. It connects households with waste collectors through a centralized system for scheduling and tracking plastic waste pickups, while providing administrators with dashboards to monitor operations and gain valuable insights. By improving coordination and making recycling easier for everyone involved, PlastiTrack aims to reduce plastic pollution, increase recycling rates, and contribute to building cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable communities.

Workmerate ERP

Visit Website

Collaborated with the lead designer to shape the UX/UI quality and led a team of developers in building Workmerate ERP—a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning & Financial Management platform. Engineered specifically to comply with the Nigeria Tax Act (NTA) 2025, the system automates tax accounting and enables seamless real-time data migration with tax authorities ahead of the 2026 compliance mandate. The platform consolidates core operational workflows—including double-entry accounting ledgers, automated payroll, inventory management, and multi-tenant billing—into a secure, high-performance web interface designed for business-critical scalability.

McSportng

Visit Website

As the UI/UX Designer on McSportng, I collaborated with the engineering team to design and build an intuitive, high-performance web platform that empowers athletes to track sport-specific performance sessions, analyze longitudinal trends, and share verified digital stat cards with coaches and scouts. The platform integrates comprehensive competition logs, video uploads, premium verification badges, adaptive metric tracking, and AI-driven performance comparisons to showcase athlete growth, improve decision-making, and unlock career opportunities in sports.
